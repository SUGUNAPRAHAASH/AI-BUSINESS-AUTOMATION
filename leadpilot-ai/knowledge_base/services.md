# BrightSmile Dental — Clinical Services & Specialties

## Preventative Care
Our preventative dentistry program prevents issues before they cause discomfort. We use low-radiation digital radiography, intraoral cameras, ultrasonic scaling, and remineralizing fluoride therapies. Every checkup includes a comprehensive oral cancer screening.

## Cosmetic Dentistry & Smile Makeovers
We specialize in non-invasive cosmetic dental makeovers:
- **Teeth Whitening:** In-office 1-hour laser whitening ($299) gives instant dramatic brightening safely without enamel erosion.
- **Porcelain Veneers:** Ultra-thin custom porcelain shells fabricated by top aesthetic labs to correct discoloration, chips, gaps, or misalignments ($950-$1,400 per tooth).
- **Composite Bonding:** Quick single-visit tooth recontouring ($220-$400).

## Orthodontics & Clear Aligners
As a certified Invisalign Platinum Provider, BrightSmile Dental straightens teeth without metal brackets or wires. Using 3D optical scanning, we preview your smile transformation before treatment begins. Full cases typically cost between $3,800 and $5,200 ($4,000 average).

## Restorative & Implant Dentistry
- **Tooth-Colored Fillings:** BPA-free composite resin blended to match your natural tooth shade ($180-$260).
- **Ceramic Dental Crowns:** Precision milled crowns that restore damaged teeth with natural aesthetics ($850-$1,200).
- **Dental Implants:** Permanent titanium and zirconia root replacements that look, feel, and function like natural teeth ($2,800-$3,900 complete).
- **Root Canal Therapy:** Gentle therapy performed with modern rotary instruments and profound local anesthesia ($650-$950).
