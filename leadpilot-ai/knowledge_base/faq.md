# BrightSmile Dental — Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### General & First Visit
1. **Q: Are you currently accepting new patients?**
   A: Yes! We are happily welcoming new patients of all ages, including individuals and families.

2. **Q: What should I expect during my first dental appointment?**
   A: Your first visit lasts approximately 60 minutes. It includes comprehensive digital diagnostic X-rays, a full periodontal health check, an oral cancer screening, a gentle cleaning, and a 1-on-1 discussion with the dentist.

3. **Q: Do I need to fill out paperwork before my visit?**
   A: You will receive an SMS and email link to complete our secure digital medical history form 24 hours prior to your visit.

4. **Q: Is BrightSmile Dental wheelchair accessible?**
   A: Yes, our medical suite features step-free elevator access, wide entryways, and ADA-compliant treatment operatories.

5. **Q: Is free parking available on-site?**
   A: Yes, free validated patient parking is available directly under the medical building. Bring your ticket to the front desk.

### Insurance & Payments
6. **Q: Which dental insurance plans do you accept?**
   A: We are in-network with Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Aetna, Guardian, and Blue Cross Blue Shield PPO plans. We also file out-of-network claims on your behalf for all other PPO insurances.

7. **Q: Do you accept Medicaid or DMO/HMO plans?**
   A: We do not accept Medicaid or HMO/DMO capitation plans. However, uninsured patients benefit from our In-House Membership Plan ($29/month).

8. **Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
   A: We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex, Discover), debit cards, HSA/FSA cards, and cash.

9. **Q: Do you offer payment plans or financing?**
   A: Yes, we offer interest-free 6-month and 12-month payment plans through CareCredit and Sunbit for treatments over $500.

10. **Q: What is the In-House Dental Membership Plan?**
    A: For $29/month, members receive 2 routine exams, 2 cleanings, annual x-rays, and 20% off all restorative, cosmetic, and orthodontic procedures with zero waiting periods and no claim maximums.

### Appointments & Cancellations
11. **Q: What is your appointment cancellation policy?**
    A: Cancellation requires 24 hours notice. Late cancellations or missed appointments without 24 hours advance notice incur a $50 fee.

12. **Q: Do you offer weekend or evening appointments?**
    A: Yes, we are open on Saturdays from 9:00 to 14:00 and offer extended evening hours on Wednesdays until 19:00.

13. **Q: Can I book back-to-back appointments for my family?**
    A: Yes, family block bookings are available on Saturday mornings and Wednesday afternoons.

14. **Q: How quickly can I get an appointment for an emergency?**
    A: We reserve daily emergency slots and can accommodate same-day emergency appointments for severe pain, trauma, or swelling.

### Cosmetic & Orthodontic Treatments
15. **Q: How much is professional teeth whitening and how long does it take?**
    A: In-office laser teeth whitening is $299. The appointment takes 60 minutes and brightens teeth by 4 to 8 shades.

16. **Q: How does Invisalign work and how much does it cost?**
    A: Invisalign uses custom clear plastic aligners to straighten teeth discreetly. Treatment costs between $3,800 and $5,200 (average $4,000). A complimentary digital 3D iTero scan is included in your consultation.

17. **Q: How long does Invisalign treatment take?**
    A: Most adult cases take between 6 and 14 months depending on the complexity of crowding or spacing.

18. **Q: What is cosmetic dental bonding?**
    A: Bonding uses tooth-colored composite resin to repair chipped teeth, close gaps, or reshape uneven edges in a single quick appointment ($220 to $400 per tooth).

19. **Q: How long do porcelain veneers last?**
    A: High-quality porcelain veneers typically last 12 to 20 years with proper oral hygiene and regular dental checkups.

### Dental Anxiety & Special Care
20. **Q: I suffer from severe dental anxiety. What options do you have?**
    A: We specialize in anxiety-free dentistry. We provide warm scented towels, noise-canceling headphones with Netflix/Spotify, painless Wand computer-controlled local anesthesia, and nitrous oxide (laughing gas).

21. **Q: Can I request nitrous oxide (laughing gas) for routine cleanings?**
    A: Yes, nitrous oxide is available for any procedure upon request for a nominal $40 fee.

22. **Q: Do you treat children and toddlers?**
    A: Yes, we provide pediatric dental care from age 2 upwards, emphasizing positive, fun, and fear-free visits.

23. **Q: What should I do if a permanent tooth is completely knocked out?**
    A: Handle the tooth only by the crown (never touch the root), gently rinse it in milk or saline without scrubbing, place it in a cup of cold milk or inside your cheek, and contact us immediately within 60 minutes.

24. **Q: How often should I visit the dentist for cleanings?**
    A: For patients with healthy gums, twice a year (every 6 months) is recommended. Patients with gum disease may require periodontal maintenance every 3 to 4 months.

25. **Q: Do you treat pets or animals (e.g. horses)?**
    A: No, BrightSmile Dental is strictly a human dental practice. We do not treat animals or pets of any kind.
