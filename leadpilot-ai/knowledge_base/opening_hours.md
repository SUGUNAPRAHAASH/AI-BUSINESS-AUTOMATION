## Location & Parking
- **Address:** 742 Evergreen Medical Suites, Suite 300, Springfield, NY 10001
- **Parking:** Dedicated patient parking spots are free in the underground lot (validated at the reception desk).
- **Public Transit:** 2 blocks from Grand Central Transit station (Line 4, 5, 6, 7).

## Holiday Closures
The clinic is closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. On Thanksgiving Eve and Christmas Eve, clinic operates with reduced hours (8:00 to 12:00).

## Dental Emergency Line & Protocol
- **During Clinic Hours:** Call the clinic desk directly at (555) 019-9000 for immediate same-day emergency triage slots.
- **After-Hours Emergencies:** Severe facial trauma, persistent bleeding, or swelling affecting breathing should be directed to the nearest Emergency Room. For severe sudden toothaches or broken teeth outside regular clinic hours, call our 24/7 patient helpline at (555) 019-9999 to speak with our on-call dental triage nurse.
