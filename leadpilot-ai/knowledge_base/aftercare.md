# BrightSmile Dental — Post-Treatment Aftercare Instructions

## Post-Teeth Whitening Care (The 48-Hour "White Diet")
For 48 hours following professional whitening, enamel pores remain open and vulnerable to staining:
- **Avoid:** Coffee, dark tea, red wine, cola, berry juices, tomato sauce, curry, soy sauce, and tobacco.
- **Allowed Foods:** Water, skim milk, plain chicken, white fish, rice, pasta with white sauce, potatoes, cauliflower, and plain yogurt.
- **Sensitivity:** Mild sensitivity is normal and resolves within 24 to 48 hours. Use potassium nitrate toothpaste (Sensodyne) if needed.

## Post-Dental Extraction Aftercare
- **Bite on Gauze:** Keep steady, firm pressure on the surgical gauze pad for 30 to 45 minutes after leaving the office.
- **Do Not Dislodge the Blood Clot:** Strictly avoid spitting, rinsing vigorously, using drinking straws, or smoking for at least 72 hours.
- **Ice Packs:** Apply ice packs to the side of the face (20 minutes on, 20 minutes off) for the first 24 hours to reduce swelling.
- **Soft Diet:** Eat soft, lukewarm foods (smoothies with a spoon, soups, applesauce, mashed potatoes) for the first 2 days.

## Post-Crown & Filling Care
- **Numbness:** Avoid chewing hard or hot foods until numbness has completely worn off to avoid biting your tongue or cheek.
- **Bite Check:** If your bite feels high or uncomfortable after the anesthesia wears off, contact us immediately for a simple 2-minute bite adjustment.
