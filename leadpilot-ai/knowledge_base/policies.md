# BrightSmile Dental — Practice Policies

## Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy
We value our patients' time and reserve dedicated operatories for each appointment.
- **Notice Requirement:** Cancellation requires 24 hours notice.
- **Late Cancellation / No-Show Fee:** Appointments cancelled or rescheduled with less than 24 hours notice, or missed appointments, are subject to a $50 administrative fee.
- **Emergency Waiver:** Cancellation fees are waived in cases of documented illness or emergencies.

## Financial & Payment Policy
- **Payment Due Date:** Payment for all co-pays, deductibles, or non-covered services is due at the time service is rendered.
- **Insurance Estimates:** Insurance quotes provided by our office are estimates based on your carrier's fee schedule. The patient remains responsible for any balance not paid by insurance within 60 days.
- **Refund Policy:** Overpayments resulting from insurance reconciliation are refunded within 14 business days via the original payment method or check. Custom laboratory restorations (crowns, veneers, Invisalign aligners) are non-refundable once sent for fabrication.

## Privacy & Patient Rights (HIPAA)
BrightSmile Dental strictly adheres to HIPAA privacy regulations. Your electronic health records, diagnostic photos, and treatment history are protected by 256-bit encryption. We never share patient information with third parties without written authorization.
